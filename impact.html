Bigger Than Sports
Scholarship

The Bigger Than Sports Scholarship provides critical financial support to students as they transition beyond high school. By covering needs like tuition, supplies, and living expenses, we help remove barriers, ease stress, and give students the freedom to chase their dreams, knowing they are seen, supported, and believed in their communities.
