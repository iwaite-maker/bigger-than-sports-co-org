Athletes lose themselves
when they lose the game.

We're here to prevent that.

Bigger Than Sports is a youth-led nonprofit fighting educational inequality through the power of sports. We match college-age mentors with student-athletes at low-income high schools to help them plan for life beyond the dream of going pro.
