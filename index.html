Athletes lose themselves

Bigger Than Sports is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to fighting educational inequality through the power of sports.

Our mission is to equip student athletes in underserved communities with the educational resources, mentorship, and structure they need to build a clear and confident plan for life after high school sports.

Learn More About What We Do

Youth-to-Youth Mentorship

The Model

We pair student athletes with near-peer mentors, young adults who are close in age and just a few years removed from their own transition out of competitive sports. Because our mentors have lived through that same crossroads, students trust them quickly and open up honestly about what comes next.

The Students

We work with student athletes at Title I high schools, many of whom have built their identity around their sport and have limited access to counselors, career guidance, or a family roadmap for what happens after the final season. They are talented, driven, and ready for a plan.

The Results

Students leave our program with a concrete plan for college, a trade, or a career, along with the confidence and structure to follow through on it. They learn to see themselves as more than an athlete, and to carry that identity into whatever comes next.

What a Year Looks Like

Interactive Modules & Self-Discovery

Students start with interactive modules designed to help them understand their own strengths, values, and interests outside of athletics, laying the groundwork for a plan that fits who they are, not just what they've played.

FAFSA & Financial Aid Navigation

Mentors walk students step by step through the FAFSA and financial aid process, demystifying paperwork that can otherwise stand between a student and their next step.

Job Search & Career Exploration

Students build real job search skills, from writing a resume to exploring trades, careers, and college paths that connect to their own goals.

Networking & Professional Skills

Through workshops and mentor connections, students practice networking and communication skills they'll use well beyond graduation.

AI Tools & Modern Career Prep

We introduce students to the AI tools that are shaping today's workplace, so they walk into their next chapter prepared rather than caught off guard.

Breaking the Athlete Identity

Throughout the year, mentors help students separate their sense of self from their stats and their jersey, so that when the game ends, they don't lose themselves along with it.
