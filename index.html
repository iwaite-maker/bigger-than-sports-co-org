Bigger Than Sports is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to fighting educational inequality through the power of sports.

Our mission is to equip student athletes in underserved communities with the educational resources, mentorship, and structure they need to build a clear and confident plan for life after high school sports.

Our ESPY® Award-Winning impact

Youth-to-Youth Mentorship

The Model

Our education program connects student athletes in their last years of high school with young-adult mentors who help them navigate college, careers, and life beyond sports.

The Students

We serve student athletes in under-resourced Title 1 schools, many of whom have built their identity around their sport and have limited access to counselors, career guidance, or a family roadmap for what happens after graduation.

The Results

Students leave our program with a concrete plan for college, a trade, or a career, along with the confidence to see themselves as more than an athlete, so that when the game ends, they know they have the tools to pursue a dream that is bigger than sports.

What the Bigger Than Sports Program Looks Like

01

Self Discovery

Before a single application gets filled out, every student works through a structured process to identify who they are beyond an athlete, learning that the discipline and resilience that make them exceptional athletes will carry them through life after sports.

02

Applications Lab

This is where the dream becomes actionable. Our trained mentors serve as personalized guidance counselors, working one-on-one with students through college applications, FAFSA completion, trade school enrollment, and scholarship and job searches.

03

Life Skills

Most students never get formal training in the skills they'll use for the rest of their lives. We train them on a range of topics, including financial literacy, strategic networking, and how to use AI tools, so they leave with a practical toolkit for whatever comes next.

04

College Visits, Guest Speakers, Field Trips & Community Events

A plan only sticks if a student can picture themselves living it. Throughout the program, we bring in industry professionals as guest speakers, take students onto college campuses and field trips, and invite them to every community event we run.

05

Hall of Fame Alumni Program

Our commitment doesn't end at graduation. Every student who completes the program joins our Hall of Fame alumni network, keeping access to mentors and resources as they move through college, a trade program, or their first career after sports.
