The Problem

If you ask any high school student-athlete if they want to go pro, plenty would say "of course." For most kids, that's not a problem. Having a dream is motivating, and when you realize it probably won't work out, you pivot. For some kids, particularly in low-income communities, getting to college on an athletic scholarship or going pro isn't a dream. It's a lifeline.

Our Solution

We exist to show student-athletes there are other options, and they already have the skills they need to pursue them. We help them find what calls to them outside of sports, then help them take tangible steps toward futures they feel excited about.

The innovative part of our approach is our youth-to-youth mentorship model. We place college-age mentors in classrooms with high school student-athletes; mentors from the same backgrounds as the students they work with who are close enough in age to create breakthroughs that traditional systems often miss.
